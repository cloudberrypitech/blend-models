# CONTRIBUTING.md

## Welcome Message
Thank you for your interest in contributing to our project! We appreciate your support and are excited to have you join our community.

## Show Your Appreciation
To help us grow and improve, please consider the following ways to show your appreciation:

## Star the Project: If you find our project useful, please give it a star on GitHub. This helps others discover the project and shows your support.
Follow the Project: Stay updated with the latest changes and releases by following our repository. You can do this by clicking the "Watch" button at the top of the repository page.
How to Contribute
We welcome contributions in various forms, including code, documentation, and feedback. Please check the sections below for more details on how you can help.

## Reporting Bugs: If you encounter any issues, please report them in the Issues section.
Suggesting Enhancements: We are open to suggestions for new features or improvements.
Submitting Code: Follow our guidelines for submitting pull requests.
